## 2026-11-01 H3 â€” multi-entry exact endpoint memo cache (expected gain written before implementation)

Hypothesis: preserve endpoint interning semantics and group storage, but add a 2,048-slot direct-mapped cache keyed by a cheap fingerprint of endpoint length, first eight bytes and final byte. Since Zipf traffic should reuse hot endpoints, this may bypass most full-string hashing while adding only 16 KiB per worker. ## 2026-11-01 H5 â€” per-worker pooled latency blocks (expected gain written before implementation)

Merge counts/errors only, then gather a group's blocks into one reusable selection scratch vector, sort/select exact percentiles and discard the scratch. This should reduce allocator churn, Vec spare capacity and group metadata without a second file scan or per-line packed-record array. Expected K gain: +8-15%, primarily from a lower anonymous-memory peak and cheaper parse-time appends; estimate only.
